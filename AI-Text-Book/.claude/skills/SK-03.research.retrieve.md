# SK-03: Research Retrieval

## Description
Retrieve relevant research papers, articles, and references from academic and technical sources. Searches databases and collections to find authoritative sources for your topic.

## Usage Example
```
/skill run SK-03.research.retrieve
Input: Research topic or keywords
Output: List of relevant papers, articles, and references with citations
```

## Key Capabilities
- Search academic databases and sources
- Filter research by relevance and recency
- Return formatted citations
- Identify key publications and authors
